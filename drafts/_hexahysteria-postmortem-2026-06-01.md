Hello players, this is meant to be a post of me (NULL) looking back at Hexa Hysteria, a mobile rhythm game I created from around 2020~2022. This is going to be a long post divided into several sections, where I analyze on the game's content and why it "failed." However, if you want to know what will happen to the game, I have prepared a short list of plans below:

- The game will remain as is; that is, there would be no more new songs added to it.
  - However, there will be one last "new story" added to the chapter 40.5 AU in the future.
- Depsite it stopping receiving new contents, the game will remain avalaible "as long as possible."
  - This depends on several factors, mostly on Unity's support and how Google Play and Apple Store change in the future.
  - However, as long as it is reasonably possible, I will try to keep the game avaiable on these two stores.
- As the game has no more plans for future content, I no longer feel comfortable charging people's money for it, especially for subscription.
  - Therefore, I will try to gradually stop the subscription service.
- Further more, I will gradually make all paid chapters free. Eventually, every piece of content in the game will be free for everyone to play.
  - There are several factors going into this decision, but the most important one is that I want to let as many people as possible to experience the effort for everyone who has worked on the game.
    - I believe the positivity this will bring outweigh whatever money I can make from keep these content paid.
  - As to the schedule of this change, I haven't landed on a concrete plan right now, but it will happen regardless
  - If you have paid for the paid chapters, and you feel unfair that these contents will become free, please contact me via contact@wiseyestudio with your Apple or Google Play account, and I will try to refund through Apple/Google's system.
    - However, Apple/Google are subject to their policies and there are a certain amount of timed window where refunds are possible. For purchases outside that window, refunds generally aren't possible through the platforms, and I'm not able to process refunds directly.
    - For everyone else: I genuinely appreciate that your past support helped this project exist at all, and I hope you'll see this change as me trying to do right by the project and its community, rather than something taken away from you.

I am sorry if this decision is disappointing. However, I simply won't be able to make back any money if I try to add more content to Hexa Hysteria. To be honest, as for now, I had already lost a good amount of money throughout the life-span of Hexa Hysteria, and I paid everything out of my pocket. There are several more factors, but unfortunately this is the ultimate truth; that I simply won't make back the money even if I try, and I can't just sink money just out of love. I will discuss this more later on.

# The future of Wiseye Studio and me
When I started Hexa Hysteria, I was in my third year in university. Looking back, the 20 year-old me was truly passionate in the project, and I sincerely thought it had the potential be successful. However, passion can also need to a good amount of delusion. Now I am 25, I am in the sudden realization that I honestly have "nothing" to show for, in that I am still just alone like when I started. I feel like I have "gained" nothing, and I didn't grow as a person or a developer. The area I have grown the most is probably my skill as an engineer; however, I am still not "good" - in all honestly, I truly believe AI has probably replaced me 95%, even after counting the growth of the past 3 years.

 However, let's focus on the positive now: Lily Fantasia will for sure receive at least four DLC updates, and I am working on them. I am also trying to write new stories and new game ideas, and I am pitching these ideas to some other more experienced teams to see if they are "worth making" or "have a chance of success." Some of them are directly related to Hexa Hysteria, but I can't share them right now.

 I am trying to have a positive look on life, but it is true that I am feeling deeply defeated right now.
